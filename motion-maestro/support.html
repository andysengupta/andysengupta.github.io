🎻 Motion Maestro — Support

Wave your phone like a conductor's baton — your movements make the music and paint the picture.

How to play

Wave faster → the tempo rises (60–160 BPM).
Bigger arcs → louder, from pianissimo to fortissimo.
Sharp flick → cymbal crash and a flash of light.
Tilt left / right → blend the dreamy pad against the melody.

Each song lasts 60 seconds, then you get a rhythm + expression score and your light-painting is saved to the Gallery.

Common questions

No sound? Check the silent switch and volume — Motion Maestro plays through the media channel.

Space Synth is locked? It unlocks at Composer Level 3 — save four performances to get there.

Where are my artworks? In the Gallery tab. They're stored only on your device; use the share button to export a PNG.
