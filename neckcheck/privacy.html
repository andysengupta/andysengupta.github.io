NeckCheck Privacy Policy

Everything stays on your device

Effective June 12, 2026

The short version

NeckCheck collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no servers. Your posture data never leaves your iPhone.

Head motion data

NeckCheck reads head-orientation data from your AirPods (via Apple's CoreMotion framework) to estimate neck posture. This data is processed in real time on your device. Summarized posture statistics (time per posture zone, scores, break counts) are stored only in the app's local database and can be deleted by deleting the app.

Calendar data

If you enable calendar smart-mute (a Pro feature), NeckCheck reads today's calendar events on-device to decide when to silence nudges. Event details are never stored, transmitted, or shared.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. NeckCheck never sees your payment details.
