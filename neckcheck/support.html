NeckCheck Support

Which headphones work?

NeckCheck needs Apple's head-tracking sensors: AirPods (3rd generation or later), AirPods Pro (any), AirPods Max, or Beats with the H1/H2 chip. Regular wired or third-party headphones don't expose head motion.

The gauge isn't moving

  • Make sure your AirPods are in your ears and connected to this iPhone.
  • Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → NeckCheck is allowed.
  • Recalibrate from the Settings tab while sitting tall.

Nudges feel too eager / too lazy

Adjust the Sensitivity slider in Settings. Relaxed widens the angles before a nudge; Strict tightens them.

Calendar smart-mute isn't muting

Smart-mute is part of NeckCheck Pro and needs full calendar access (Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars). It mutes during events that have attendees or look like calls/meetings.
