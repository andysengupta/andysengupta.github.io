🚀 Cosmo Quiz

Cosmo Quiz — Support

Cosmo Quiz mixes a 300-question science and space quiz with three original arcade games, built for curious kids aged 10 to 12. There is no sign-up, no adverts, and nothing to buy — open it and play.

FAQ

What is in the app?

Three arcade games — Space Adventures (dodge asteroids, collect stars), Asteroid Blaster (a sixty-second shooting challenge) and Brick Breaker — plus a 300-question quiz across Easy, Medium and Hard difficulties, 100 science jokes, and a scoreboard with six achievements to unlock.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on the device. Cosmo Quiz makes no network requests at all, so it works on a plane, in a car, or anywhere else.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Cosmo Quiz is completely free, with no adverts, no in-app purchases, and no accounts.

Where are scores kept?

On the device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes the scores with it.

How are the quiz questions chosen?

Each round draws ten questions at the difficulty you pick. Points scale with difficulty, so harder questions are worth more.

My child found a question that looks wrong. What should I do?

Please email us the question and we will correct it. Accuracy matters more to us than the size of the question bank, and we would rather hear about it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later, in portrait orientation.
