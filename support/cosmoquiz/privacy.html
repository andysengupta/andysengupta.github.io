Cosmo Quiz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Cosmo Quiz collects nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The app makes no network requests of any kind, so no information ever leaves your device. We operate no servers.

What we collect

Nothing at all. There is no data collection in this app — not anonymous, not aggregated, not optional.

What is stored on your device

Your quiz scores, points, rounds played and unlocked achievements are saved in the app's own storage on your device so your progress is there when you come back. This stays on the device, is never transmitted, and is deleted along with the app if you remove it.

Children's privacy

Cosmo Quiz is designed for children aged around 10 to 12. It does not collect, store, or share personal information from anyone, including children. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases, no links out to the web, no social features, and no way for anyone to contact a child through the app.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics provider, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no cloud service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
