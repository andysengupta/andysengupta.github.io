After-Glow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

After-Glow is built to calm you down, not to watch you. The whole policy in one sentence: everything happens on your device and nothing is collected, stored on our servers, or shared — because we have no servers.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. After-Glow has no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We never receive your location, your calendar, your health data, or anything else.

Location

If — and only if — you turn on a location-based trigger (for example, "offer my arriving-home ritual when I get home"), After-Glow uses iOS region monitoring to notice when you enter or leave a place you chose. This is low-power geofencing, not continuous tracking. Your location is evaluated on your device to decide whether to suggest a ritual, and is never stored by us or transmitted anywhere. You can disable it any time in the app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Calendar

If you enable calendar-based triggers, After-Glow reads your calendar on your device only to detect when a meeting has just ended, so it can offer a reset ritual. Event details are processed in the moment and are never copied off your device, stored by us, or shared.

Apple Health

When you complete a ritual, After-Glow can write the time as Mindful Minutes to Apple Health, if you grant permission. That data is written into your own Apple Health store, which you control — it is not sent to us. After-Glow does not read your other health data.

Notifications

"Time to reset" suggestions are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No information leaves your phone to deliver them.

Data storage

Your settings, chosen transitions, and streaks are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We keep no copy of anything.

Children's privacy

After-Glow does not collect, store, or share personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
