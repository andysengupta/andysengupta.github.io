🌅 After-Glow

After-Glow — Support

After-Glow helps you reset in the moments between things — after a hard meeting, before you walk in your front door, between work and sleep. It notices those transitions and offers a two-minute ritual: a guided breath, a reflective prompt, and a physical cue. Everything runs on your device.

FAQ

How does it know when to suggest a ritual?

If you enable smart triggers, After-Glow can offer the right ritual when you arrive home (using low-power location geofencing) or when a calendar meeting ends. Both are evaluated on your device and never leave it. You can also start any ritual manually, any time.

Why does it ask for Location, Calendar, or Health permission?

Each is optional and only used for the feature you turn on: Location for arriving-home/work triggers, Calendar to detect when a meeting ends, and Apple Health to log completed rituals as Mindful Minutes. None of this data is collected or sent anywhere. Manage permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Do I have to enable location?

No. After-Glow works fully with manual rituals and reminders. Location and calendar triggers are conveniences you can switch on if you want the app to show up at the right moment on its own.

Did I lose my settings or streak?

Everything is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it, and there's no account or cloud sync, so it doesn't transfer to a new device.

Is there anything to buy?

After-Glow is free to use. A future optional subscription (After-Glow+) may unlock smart triggers and custom rituals; core rituals stay free.
