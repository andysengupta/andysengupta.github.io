Frequently asked questions

How do I add or change medicines?

Open Family setup (the small button at the top of the Today screen). There you can add medicines, choose how each pill looks (shape, colour, size), set the routine anchors ("after breakfast"), and manage the family circle. Tap Done — show the garden view to hand the phone back.

What do the flowers mean?

🌼 a blooming flower = a well-taken day · 🌷 a tulip = taken late, still a flower · 🌱 a closed bud = a missed day. Never red, never guilt — the garden simply grows with you.

Why does Daisy say "already taken"?

That's the double-dose protection: each routine slot can only be confirmed once per day, so a moment of forgetfulness can never count a medicine twice.

Are reminders private?

Yes. All reminders are scheduled locally on the device. Daisy has no server and collects no data — see the privacy policy.

Is Daisy a medical device?

No. Daisy is a routine companion. It gives no medical, dosage or interaction advice and never replaces the guidance of your doctor or pharmacist.
