🌼 Daisy Medicine Garden — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026 · App: Daisy Medicine Garden (iOS)

The short version: Daisy collects nothing. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no server. Your medicine list, routine and garden live only on your device.

What data we collect

None. Daisy does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app:

  • requires no account, sign-up, email address or phone number;
  • contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no third-party tracking of any kind;
  • stores your medicine list, routine anchors, family-circle names and garden history only on your device;
  • never sells, rents or shares data with anyone — there is nothing to share.

Health-related information

The medicine names, nicknames and schedules you (or a family member) type into Daisy stay on the device. They are never uploaded anywhere by us. Daisy is a routine companion, not a medical device: it gives no medical, dosage or interaction advice, and it does not connect to pharmacies, doctors or health services.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Daisy schedules gentle local reminders on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device.

Sharing your garden

The optional "Send my garden" postcard uses the standard iOS share sheet. You choose if, how and with whom an image is shared — Daisy itself sends nothing.

Children's privacy

Daisy is designed for older adults and their families. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all of its data, because all of its data lives inside the app on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Daisy ever changes any of the above (for example, optional iCloud family sharing), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly explained in the app.
