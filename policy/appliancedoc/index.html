Privacy Policy

The short version: your appliance's vibration and sound never leave your iPhone. We hold no servers with your sensor data, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The only personal data involved is the sign-in identity you choose to create.

Sensor data stays on your device

During a scan, ApplianceDoc records vibration (accelerometer) and audio (microphone) while your phone rests on the appliance. All signal processing — spectral analysis, fault classification, trend tracking — runs entirely on your iPhone. Raw sensor recordings are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. Diagnostic results and scan history are stored locally on your device.

Account information

If you create an account, sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google LLC service). Depending on the method you choose — email/password, Google, or Sign in with Apple — Firebase processes your email address, display name, and a unique user identifier. With Sign in with Apple you may use Apple's private email relay. We use this information solely to authenticate you. See Firebase's privacy documentation for Google's handling of authentication data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

On-device intelligence

AI-generated explanations and the "Ask the Mechanic" chat run on-device using Apple's Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). Your questions and scan results are not sent to any server.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored scan data. To delete your authentication record, email anandraj.sengupta@icloud.com from the address associated with your account.

Terms of Service

Advisory diagnostics only

ApplianceDoc provides informational, advisory diagnostics based on vibration and acoustic analysis. It is not a certified inspection, safety assessment, or professional repair service. Diagnoses carry confidence estimates and may be wrong. For gas appliances, electrical faults, water leaks, or any safety-critical concern, consult a qualified technician immediately.

Subscriptions

ApplianceDoc Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Free-trial periods, where offered, convert to a paid subscription unless cancelled before the trial ends.

Acceptable use

Use ApplianceDoc only on appliances you own or have permission to assess. Pre-purchase reports reflect a single moment of operation and do not guarantee an appliance's future condition.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, ApplianceDoc and its developer are not liable for damages arising from reliance on diagnostic results, including repair decisions, purchase decisions, or appliance failures. The app is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind.
