Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Our mobile applications are designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary for app functionality:

  • Motion Data: Accelerometer and gyroscope data for ride tracking (processed locally on your device)
  • Usage Data: App performance metrics and crash reports to improve user experience
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for compatibility

How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for:

  • Providing core app functionality (ride tracking, smoothness scoring)
  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Debugging technical issues and crashes
  • Ensuring app compatibility across different devices
Important: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. All motion data is processed locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on your device using iOS Keychain and UserDefaults. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple CoreMotion: For motion sensor data (no data sharing)
  • Crashlytics: For crash reporting (anonymized data only)
