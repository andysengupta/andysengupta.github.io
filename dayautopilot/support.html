Day Autopilot — Support

Day Autopilot is a zero-input time journal. Your iPhone's motion history, learned places and barometer reconstruct your day on-device — desk time, meetings, commutes, walks — and write your standup note for you.

FAQ

Why is my timeline empty?
Day Autopilot needs Motion & Fitness and Location (Always) permissions to journal passively. Check Settings → Day Autopilot. With motion-only access you'll still get desk/walk blocks, without place names or commutes.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics. Deleting the app deletes everything; there is also an "Erase everything" button in the Privacy tab.

A block is labeled wrong.
Tap it and pick the right label. Your corrections are never overwritten.

What does Pro include?
Unlimited history, CSV export, custom standup templates and floor-change detail — a one-time $14.99 purchase, no subscription.
