Day Autopilot — Privacy Policy

Effective 12 June 2026

Day Autopilot collects no data. Not "minimal data" — none.

What the app senses

  • Motion activity (walking, stationary, driving) via Apple's Core Motion framework
  • Location visits to learn places like Home and Office
  • Barometric pressure changes to detect floor changes

Where it goes

Nowhere. All sensing, classification and storage happens on your device. The app makes no network connections, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and has no accounts or servers. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Your control

  • Each sensor can be toggled off in the Privacy tab.
  • "Erase everything" permanently deletes all journal data.
  • Deleting the app deletes all data with it.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information about you.
